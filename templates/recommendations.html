Operational Guidelines & Best Practices

To ensure reliable and scientifically accurate forensic decisions, adhere to the following input conditions.

1. Supported Target Categories

The model was trained on the balanced MD-SynReal12K dataset covering:

  • Human Faces: Close-up and portrait facial structures.
  • Nature & Biological Fauna: Terrestrial/marine wildlife and organic plants.
  • Artificial Objects: Vehicles, indoor furniture, electronic appliances, and tools.
Unrelated documents, charts, and low-contrast textures trigger OOD rejection.
2. Image Resolution & Compression

Frequency artifacts can be attenuated by excessive lossy compression.

  • Upload original, uncompressed captures when possible.
  • Minimum recommended resolution: 224 × 224 pixels.
  • Heavy social-media recompression (e.g., WhatsApp downscaling) may lower spectral confidence below the 80% boundary.
3. Interpreting Confidence & OOD Alert

The web app enforces a strict 80% threshold:

  • ≥ 80%: High confidence verdict (Authentic vs. AI-Generated).
  • < 80%: Marked as Out of Distribution (OOD). The image falls outside the training manifold or exhibits contradictory spatial/spectral clues.
4. Real-Time Deployment Constraints

Engineered for edge forensic workstations:

  • Average latency is 10–15 ms on GPU and ~45 ms on modern multi-core CPU.
  • Memory footprint: ~25 MB weight storage with minimal VRAM consumption.
