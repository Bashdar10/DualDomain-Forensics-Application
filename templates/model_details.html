Dual-Domain Attention Fusion Overview

The framework processes the input image through two orthogonal pathways:

  • Spatial Stream (EfficientNet-B0): Extracts localized texture patterns, biological facial geometry, and structural contour features (1,280-dimensional embedding).
  • Frequency Stream (Swin-Tiny Transformer): Computes 2D Discrete Fast Fourier Transform (2D FFT) magnitude representations to detect periodic upsampling lattices, grid artifacts, and spectral phase decay (768-dimensional embedding).
  • Dynamic Attention Gate: Unified 2,048-dim vectors are dynamically weighted through a multi-layer perceptron ($\text{Linear} \to \text{ReLU} \to \text{Linear} \to \text{Sigmoid}$) to prioritize the most discriminative domain before classification.
